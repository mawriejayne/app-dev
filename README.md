

# Bridge to Terabithia

> "Terabithia was a secret kingdom, a place where you could be anyone you wanted to be."

## About

Bridge to Terabithia is a fantasy drama film released in 2007. It is based on the 1977 novel by Katherine Paterson, which won the Newbery Medal in 1978. The story follows two lonely kids who build an imaginary kingdom in the woods and learn about friendship, courage, and loss.

## Main Characters

- Jess Aarons (Josh Hutcherson): a quiet, artistic boy who loves to draw
- Leslie Burke (AnnaSophia Robb): a creative, imaginative new girl in town
- May Belle Aarons (Bailee Madison): Jess's younger sister
- Miss Edmunds (Zooey Deschanel): the music teacher Jess admires
- Jack Aarons (Robert Patrick): Jess's father

## Plot Summary

1. Jess Aarons is a boy who feels like an outsider at school and at home.
2. Leslie Burke moves in next door, and the two become best friends.
3. Together they create Terabithia, an imaginary kingdom in the woods across a creek.
4. In Terabithia, they face imaginary creatures and become its king and queen.
5. A tragedy changes Jess's life, and he learns to honor Leslie's memory.

## Themes

- Friendship: Jess and Leslie help each other grow.
- Imagination: Terabithia shows how creativity can help people through hard times.
- Grief and Healing: the story shows how people cope with losing someone.
- Courage: Jess learns to face his fears.

## Why I Like It
